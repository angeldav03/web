Workflow

  1. Open raw 360_xxxx.JPG / .MP4 files from the camera's SD card (several at once is fine).
  2. Look around: drag, scroll to zoom. Check the seams with O.
  3. If the seam is visible, press Auto-align seam and Match exposure, or fine-tune by hand. Settings are remembered per camera model.
  4. Convert & save writes an equirectangular JPEG/MP4 with 360° metadata (GPano XMP for photos, Spherical Video V1+V2 for videos).

Tips

  • Gear 360 files carry a factory lens calibration and a tilt sensor reading; both are used automatically.
  • Objects closer than ~1 m to the camera show parallax at the seam; no calibration can fully fix that.
  • Videos are decoded, stitched and encoded on your GPU. Chrome or Edge give the best codec support (HEVC input).
